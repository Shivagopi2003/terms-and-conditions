## 3. Description of Service

Roynix Security is an automated Discord security and moderation system designed to protect Discord communities from malicious actions, unauthorized modifications, raids, and security breaches. Core capabilities include:
* **Anti-Nuke Protection:** Automated detection and mitigation of unauthorized channel deletion, role tampering, member bans/kicks, webhook creations, and guild modifications.
* **Admin Secure & Zero-Trust Quarantine:** Rapid revocation of administrative privileges when untrusted or unauthorized bots are introduced.
* **Content & Link Security:** In-memory screening against known phishing domains, malicious scam lures, executable attachments, and message flooding.
* **Blueprint Recovery:** Automated restoration and reconstruction of deleted server assets (channels, roles, permission overwrites).
* **Incident Observability & Diagnostics:** Logging of security events, executor attribution, and threat scoring.
